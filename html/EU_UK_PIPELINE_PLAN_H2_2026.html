Context Engineering Campaign

Targets accounts that have modernised — or were always cloud-native — but whose AI and agentic projects are stalling due to missing semantic foundations. Technology-neutral: the context layer can be built on any cloud stack the customer or partner prefers.

Unified Campaign — "From Data to Context" / "AI Agents Need a Foundation"
Synthetic POC Library — 5 Pre-Built Verticals

Each POC uses a small synthetic dataset (200–1,000 rows, 3–5 entities). The demo moment is explicit: run the same query without the context layer (fails or hallucinates), then with it (precise answer). That contrast is the sell.

VerticalDomainSynthetic EntitiesAgent Queries (the demo)Why Context Layer is the Unlock
Credit Risk Desk BFSI — Retail Bank Customers · Loan accounts · Transaction summaries · Credit events · Collateral "Which customers share behavioural patterns with our 2023 early-default cohort?" / "For watch-list customers, show average utilisation trend over 6 months" "Early-default cohort" and "watch-list" are derived semantic classifications — not columns in any source table. Without the context layer the agent cannot resolve them.
AML / Transaction Monitoring BFSI — Universal Bank Customers · Transactions · Beneficiary network · Alert history · Typology tags "Summarise customers flagged in 60 days who share a beneficiary with a previously closed SAR case" / "Which accounts show high-cash-deposit pattern followed by dormancy, then sudden outbound activity?" "Shared beneficiary" and "SAR linkage" span three source systems. Without the context layer, the join is impossible without bespoke SQL — the agent has no path to the answer.
Insurance Claims Intelligence BFSI — P&C Insurer Policies · Claims · Claimants · Loss events · Adjuster notes · Settlement history "Which open motor claims show characteristics historically linked to litigation? Rank by exposure." / "Compare average settlement time for weather-related claims across two regions" "Litigation characteristics" and "weather-related" are semantic tags applied at ingestion. No source system stores them as columns — they only exist in the context layer.
Network Quality + Customer Experience Telco — Mobile Operator Cell towers · Daily KPIs (throughput, latency, RSRP) · Maintenance tickets · Customer complaints "Which towers have had degraded throughput for more than 5 days AND an open maintenance ticket older than 48 hours?" / "For the top complaint-generating towers, what is the average time from degradation onset to ticket creation?" "Degraded throughput" is a derived threshold over raw time-series KPIs — it does not exist as a flag in any source. Without the context layer, the agent has no concept of "degradation."
B2B Account Health Telco — Enterprise Enterprise accounts · Contracted services · SLA records · Incident log · Usage trends · Renewal dates "Which accounts are within 90 days of renewal, have had 2+ SLA breaches, and show declining usage?" / "For managed SD-WAN accounts, what is average incident-to-resolution time vs SLA commitment?" "Renewal proximity," "SLA breach count," and "usage trend" live in CRM, ticketing, and usage systems — three separate domains. The context layer is the only thing that connects them into a single answerable question.
