Outward — market-facing GTM & delivery

Track 4

Platform Co-Sell Engine

Outward
  • Q1Register CE positioning in Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, and AWS co-sell programs — not as a generic SI, but as a Context Engineering specialist. Platform field reps need to know KPI by name when a CE deal comes up.
  • Q1Joint solution brief with each platform: "KPI + Databricks for Insurance CE", "KPI + Snowflake for CE", "KPI + Microsoft Fabric for CE". These are the one-pagers platform AEs hand to customers asking about CE.
  • Q2First co-sell pipeline: 3 warm leads from platform partners by end of Q2. Platform AEs are incentivised to close — use that.
  • Q3Databricks Marketplace listing for the CE Assessment SKU. Formalise Anthropic partnership (CE builds on Claude — Anthropic has a certified partner program). Milestone

Track 5

CE Assessment SKU — The Wedge

Outward
  • Q1Define the CE Readiness Assessment: 4 weeks, fixed-fee (~$30–40K). Deliverable: CE Maturity Score, one Quick Win, 90-day roadmap. Hunters sell this — no presales dependency.
  • Q2Deliver first assessment with a friendly or partner-referred customer. Build a template so junior consultants can run it independently.
  • Q2–3Target 4–6 assessments by end of Q3. Each one creates a qualified view into whether a vertical accelerator is warranted — the assessment is the due diligence, not just a sales step.
  • Q4Assessment runs without the CE lead's direct involvement. If that's not true by Q4, the SKU isn't productised enough yet.

Inward — capability building, practice discipline & quality gate

Track 1

KPI Asset Manager — SA-Grade Context for Every Seller

Inward

KAM is the primary lever for sales scale without big headcount scale in Presales or Delivery. Today, every competitive question, first SOW draft, or meeting prep pulls a presales SA or a delivery architect into the loop. KAM removes that dependency for routine motions — hunters self-serve in minutes, Presales & Delivery focus on qualified deals.

  • Q1Establish SharePoint taxonomy structured so an LLM can understand the purpose of each artifact and re-use it for context-building in similar future engagements — e.g. a proposal stored as [year]-[vertical]-[customer]-[topic] signals type, domain, and audience at a glance. Upload first 50 assets — existing proposals, estimation templates, capability decks.
  • Q1–2Connect Claude and Copilot to KAM via MCP. Any hunter queries "give me our last three insurance proposals" and gets a draft in minutes — minimal presales / engineering dependency.
  • Q2–3CE capability decks onboarded: Databricks, Snowflake, Fabric, AWS. Each new outgoing proposal auto-archived and indexed as it ships.
  • Q3KAM live for all hunters and farmers. Establish Proposal Velocity KPI: time-to-first-draft before vs. after KAM. Milestone
  • Q4KAM self-evolving: sales managers query win/loss patterns, hunters pull vertical playbooks without presales involvement.

5 no-queue, no-SA-needed seller scenarios

APre-meeting deck, 5 minutes

“Build me 4 slides — insurance, agents can't access claims during calls, Databricks estate, meeting at 10am tomorrow.”

KAM pulls the insurance CE accelerator spec, the nearest reference engagement, and the platform architecture. Seller tweaks 2 slides and walks in tailored — not generic.

BCompetitive compare, on the fly

“Fractal vs KPI for insurance CE — what's our angle? Prospect just mentioned them.”

KAM surfaces differentiation from past proposals and outputs a 3-bullet talking card. Seller sends it as a same-day follow-up — no presales call needed.

CSOW first draft, no queue

“CE assessment scope — retail bank, Databricks, ~200 data engineers. Prospect wants a ballpark today.”

KAM pulls the last 3 similar scopes and outputs a rough SOW in 20 minutes. What used to wait 3 days for presales ships the same day.

DCold outreach, not a template

“Outreach note — CPG on Snowflake, supply chain visibility came up in their last earnings call.”

KAM finds the supply chain CE case study and writes a 5-line email to that specific problem — not a generic intro that reads like every other vendor pitch.

ECXO prep, 30 mins before

“What will a banking CDO ask about CE implementation risk and data lineage? Meeting in 30 mins.”

KAM surfaces objection patterns from past BCBS/RDARR proposals and produces a Q&A sheet. Seller walks into the hard questions prepared — not just prepared for the demo.

Inward

Track 9 · Presales

Presales Excellence & Artifact Quality

Inward

Presales is the vertical thread at every stage — it owns the artifact, sets the winning theme, and pulls from TA + Delivery Head. Gate sequence: TA + Delivery Head → Presales (owns) → Sales & Leadership (feedback + sign-off) → Partner → Customer.

  • Q1Define "A-grade proposal" standard — publish the gate flow in writing. Build TA and Delivery Head review checklists. Name owners at every stage. First pipeline run with real pursuits.
  • Q2First mock pitch: SAs present to a simulated CDO/CTO panel; sellers play client. Subject Depth Library in KAM: 10 industry primers (BCBS 239, RDARR, IFRS 17, FHIR R4, IRB risk…). First external SA appearance. Milestone
  • Q3Proposal quality KPI live: win rate tracked by pipeline compliance vs. skipped stages. 2–3 SAs presenting externally at partner events or Databricks/Snowflake field sessions.
  • Q4Win-rate retro: gated vs. skipped proposals — data informs 2028 standards. Speaker pipeline formalised: Live Wire → mock pitch → webinar panel → conference breakout. Subject Depth Library at 15 primers. Milestone

Track 7

Practice Discipline — What We Build, What We Decline

Inward
  • Q1Define ICP: the customer KPI is building CE for. Minimum criteria — large data estate on Databricks, Snowflake, or Microsoft Fabric, a CDO or analytics VP, operates in insurance, banking, or healthcare. Turn down work that doesn't fit.
  • Q1Publish kill criteria for accelerators in writing: 0 qualified pilot conversations within a tangible predefined timeline = retired. No exceptions — take the lesson and build a better pilot. This protects engineering bandwidth and forces honest signal from the market.
  • Q2Quarterly accelerator review: active, paused, or killed. One slide per accelerator reviewed with the CE lead and a sponsor. Killing early is the discipline — not the failure. Cadence
  • Q2–3Say no to staff augmentation requests in CE-adjacent areas. Tredence and LatentView both exited aug pricing deliberately — it crowds out IP build and signals commodity positioning.
  • Q3Document what KPI has declined: specific project types, deal structures, and verticals that don't fit. Used in sales to frame the practice as a specialist, not as a filter. Milestone
  • Q42027 accelerator retrospective: what got traction, what got killed, what surprised us. This is the most honest signal on whether CE is working — and feeds the 2028 plan.

Track 8

Always-on Mechanisms — Internal Team

Inward

Both mechanisms close the gap between technical delivery and commercial awareness. [Syntax to Semantics] builds business vocabulary — architects who read market signals stop thinking like engineers and start thinking like advisors. Live Wire is the practice surface — presenting real work to a room of sellers, handling commercial questions live, distilling a complex build into three slides. These are the skills that turn a great architect into someone who can open doors, shape a deal, and eventually lead a practice.

MS Teams Channel

KPI [Syntax to Semantics]

SA · Principal Architect · Directors · Always open

The channel where KPI's senior delivery community tracks what is moving in the semantic and agentic space — before it reaches a client conversation. Platform releases, patterns that worked on engagements, papers worth ten minutes, failures that saved a project.

Who is in it

SA and above · Directors only. No sellers. No leadership. Invite-only, stays that way. Anyone in the group posts when they have something worth sharing — no curation gate, no assigned owner.

  • Ambient awareness across the senior technical team
  • Searchable record of what KPI was tracking and building
  • Direct feed into Live Wire — best channel signals become the Current segment each session
  • After every Live Wire, the presenting architect drops a 3-line summary and slides here

Bi-weekly · 45 min

KPI Live Wire

All architects · All sellers · Practice lead facilitates

The session where real client and pilot work is presented to the full team — delivery and sellers in the same room. Turns internal builds into market offers. Surfaces the architects who can sell.

SlotTimeWhat happens
Current5 minPractice lead: 2–3 live signals from the agentic space. No slides.
Wire30 min1–2 architects present real client or pilot work with full technical depth. Sellers ask questions live.
Distil5 minSame architect produces a 3-slide seller summary on the spot. This is the test.
Spark5 minSellers: "I have a prospect with this pain." Architects respond. Practice lead notes every match.
The 3-slide rule: Full technical depth in the Wire. Three seller-ready slides in the Distil. The ability to distil your own work into three slides is the signal that an architect is ready for client-facing responsibility.
  • Live Wire Log in [Syntax to Semantics]: every session logged with seller interest and packaging status — Unpackaged → In progress → Offer ready
  • Items that reach Offer Ready go into KAM and into the CCO seller kit
