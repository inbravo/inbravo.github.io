The BI Disruption — Next 18 Months

Every AI BI surface is only as trustworthy as the semantic foundation underneath it. This is the demand signal for CE methodology.

The Convergence Argument

Agentic data engineering is the synthesis of two simultaneous disruptions — not one. Understanding both is what makes the CE + IE methodology argument commercially precise rather than generic AI hype.

Disruption 1 — DE Workflows
2023–24: AI-assisted DE — LLMs writing SQL, dbt models, documentation. Human still in the loop.
2025→: Agentic DE — agents autonomously orchestrating, repairing, and adapting pipelines without human steering.
Disruption 2 — BI Consumption
2025–26: The output contract for data engineers has changed. Data is no longer built for dashboards — it is built for AI-native interfaces (Genie, Claude Dashboards, Cortex Analyst). AI-available ≠ AI-consumable.
Enabling Force — Semantic Infra
2025–26: Semantic layers are becoming cloud defaults (Unity Catalog, Snowflake Horizon, Fabric Semantic Models). The substrate exists. CE is the practice that makes it your semantic layer, not the platform's generic one.

The implication: Every client on Databricks or Fabric is sitting at this intersection — agentic DE is arriving from above, BI disruption is arriving from below. The engineering discipline that connects them is CE + IE. That is your white space.

Quick Starts — Four Door-Openers

Each exposes a specific gap the client hasn't yet quantified. Maximum 2 weeks. QS-3 is the strongest — every regulated enterprise fails it on first pass. QS-4 can run before a deal is even close.

QS-1
Semantic Debt Scorecard
2 weeks · Fixed fee
"What percentage of your metric definitions will Genie or Claude Dashboards get wrong?"
Deliverable: Scored conflict map of metric definitions across your Databricks estate. Benchmark: dbt semantic layer achieves 83% NL accuracy vs ~40% without it — clients see exactly where they sit. Every enterprise with Databricks + Power BI has this gap today. Natural lead into Accelerators A, B, or C.
QS-2
AI Cost-per-Answer Benchmark
2 weeks · Fixed fee
"Do you know your cost-per-business-answer from your AI investments?"
Deliverable: Cost-per-answer baseline for top 3 AI use cases + model routing recommendation + Unity AI Gateway config baseline. The CFO question nobody has answered. Gartner: agentic workloads cost 5–30× more than chatbot calls — this diagnostic makes that visible. No vertical bias, qualifies every enterprise with live agents.
QS-3
Accountability Gap Audit
2 weeks · Fixed fee · Strongest door-opener
"If your AI agent made a wrong decision today — who would know, and by when?"
Deliverable: Accountability Gap Map — which AI decisions are auditable, which have stop rules, which have cost visibility, and which have nothing. For BFSI: scored against BCBS 239 and DORA requirements. Every regulated enterprise fails this audit on first pass. Directly mirrors your positioning. Natural lead into IE Sprint and Spec Engineering.
QS-4
Intent Map Workshop
2 days · Fixed fee · Pre-sale viable
"Can you describe what 'done' looks like for each of your AI agents?"
Deliverable: Intent Gap Heat Map for top 3 agents — surfaces missing stop rules, undefined success criteria, unbounded autonomy. Opens with the Klarna case: perfect context, zero intent encoded → $60M later, had to rehire. Two days means this can run before a deal is even close. Fastest foot in the door you have.
What NOT to Lead With — Research-Backed Caution
  • Don't lead with "we do semantic layers" — cloud vendors ship this as infrastructure now. Genie Ontology, Horizon Context, Fabric Semantic Models are all GA in 2026.
  • Don't lead with "we help you migrate to Databricks" — short-lived story; soon all customers will be migrated. Target those already on cloud who need the layer above the platform.
  • Don't lead with generic "agentic AI" — Wipro, TCS, and every large SI says this. It is table stakes noise in October 2026.
  • Don't describe CE or IE as a "layer" — they are methodologies. A methodology cannot be commoditized; a layer can.
