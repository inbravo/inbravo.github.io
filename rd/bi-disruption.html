§1 — The Setup

Two disruptions. One intersection.

This isn't one market shift. It's two happening simultaneously — and where they cross is the opportunity.

The Convergence Argument

Agentic data engineering is the synthesis of two simultaneous disruptions — not one. Understanding both is what makes the methodology argument commercially precise rather than generic AI hype.

Disruption 1 — DE Workflows
2023–24: AI-assisted DE — LLMs writing SQL, dbt models, documentation. Human still in the loop.
2025→: Agentic DE — agents autonomously orchestrating, repairing, and adapting pipelines without human steering.
Disruption 2 — BI Consumption
2025–26: The output contract for data engineers has changed. Data is no longer built for dashboards — it is built for AI-native interfaces (Genie, Claude Dashboards, Cortex Analyst). AI-available ≠ AI-consumable.
Enabling Force — Semantic Infra
2025–26: Semantic layers are becoming cloud defaults — Unity Catalog, Snowflake Horizon, Fabric Semantic Models. The substrate exists. The practice that makes it your semantic layer — not the platform's generic one — is what's missing.

The implication: Every client on Databricks or Fabric is sitting at this intersection — agentic DE arriving from above, BI disruption arriving from below. The engineering discipline that connects them is CE + IE. That is your white space.

§2 — The Architecture

BI is splitting into three layers

The tools that bundled extraction, semantic logic, and rendering together for 20 years are losing that advantage. Each layer is now a separate market — and Impetus plays across all three.

★ Layer 2 — Semantic Foundation
Governed Metric Definitions — Defined Once, Served Everywhere
★ Impetus Anchor

KPIs unlocked in Layer 1 land here — defined once in YAML or MetricFlow as revenue, churn, risk exposure, regulatory capital — and served to any renderer via REST, GraphQL, or MCP. The semantic layer is the durable asset. Metric governance is ongoing, not a one-time project. This is where the client relationship deepens.

Context Engineering applies here

CE encodes not just what a KPI is, but what it means to the business — units, grain, filters, edge cases, business calendar rules. IE adds intent: when an agent should trust a metric, when to escalate, what stop rules apply. Without CE + IE at this layer, NL-to-SQL produces confident wrong answers on real enterprise estates.

§4 — The New Interaction Paradigm

Vibe Analytics

The term is new. The pattern is accelerating.

"Vibe Analytics" — MIT Sloan / Michael Schrage

Coined by MIT Sloan's Michael Schrage (MIT Sloan Management Review, 2025). The core idea: eliminate the traditional translation layer between business questions and technical analysis. The user describes what they want to an LLM — the tool builds the visualisation, exploration, or app. No SQL. No analyst queue. No waiting three days for the wrong chart.

It's the analytics version of vibe coding: the user's intent becomes the interface.

"A Southeast Asian telecom surfaced more financially relevant insights in 90 minutes than it typically generates in 90 days."

MIT Sloan Management Review, 2026

"MCP tools gave agents a standard way to discover schemas and run queries — vibe analytics as MCP-native analytics."

MotherDuck / Vercel framing, 2026
Why Vibe Analytics Needs the Semantic Layer

Most vibe analytics demos work on a clean star schema with five tables. Enterprise estates have 50,000 tables, three conflicting definitions of "revenue," and a semantic layer nobody has maintained since 2019. A governed semantic model is the only thing that makes Genie, Cortex Analyst, and Fabric Copilot safe to put in front of an executive. Almost none are. That is the entry point.

§7 — The Portable BI Play

The experience is leaving the tool

The lock-in that Tableau, Power BI, and Qlik rely on lives in their semantic layer. Move that layer out, make it portable, and the BI tool becomes a rendering surface you can swap. OSI is early. The opportunity to own the portability layer is now.

Most Mature
dbt Semantic Layer (MetricFlow)

Define metrics once in YAML alongside dbt models. MetricFlow generates SQL for every downstream tool. Flows to Tableau Cloud, Power BI, Looker, Genie via REST, GraphQL, and SQL. Requires paid dbt Starter or Enterprise tier.
